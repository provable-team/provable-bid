Maya Okafor, business card

Trim size: 3.5 × 2 in (US standard business card). Both sides are shown below.

To print: open the print dialog, choose the actual size or 100% scale with no “fit to page” scaling, and turn off headers and footers. Page 1 is the front and page 2 is the back. Print on card stock and cut along the trim size.
